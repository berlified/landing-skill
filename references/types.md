# Types — 11 page goals

TYPE decides sections, copy and CTA. Layout and style never change this.

| # | id | Name | Goal | Sections (in order) | Headline formula | Primary CTA |
|---|---|------|------|---------------------|------------------|-------------|
| 1 | `lead-gen` | Lead Generation | capture qualified lead | nav · hero(form+benefits) · logos · benefits(3) · proof · FAQ(3) · final CTA · footer | "Get [result] without [pain]" | Get free [audit/template/demo] → form |
| 2 | `click-through` | Click-Through Pre-sell | warm click to checkout | nav · hero · problem(3 pains) · solution · plans teaser · testimonial · CTA → pricing | "[Product] is the fastest way to [result]" | Continue → /pricing |
| 3 | `sales` | Sales Long-form | sell on the page | nav · hero · logos · problem→agitate · solution · how(3 steps) · features(6) · proof · pricing · guarantee · FAQ(6) · final CTA | "Finally, [result] in [timeframe]" | Buy / Start trial |
| 4 | `squeeze` | Squeeze / Email Capture | email only, no nav | headline · subhead · email field · bullets(3) · micro-proof | "Free [thing]: [result] in [time]" | Send it to me → form |
| 5 | `waitlist` | Coming Soon / Waitlist | notify-me + position | logo · teaser visual · headline · email + position counter · share hint · footer | "[Product] launches [when]. Get early access." | Join waitlist → form |
| 6 | `viral` | Pre-launch Viral / Referral | shares | hero + position · how referrals work(3) · rewards(3 tiers) · leaderboard teaser · share buttons | "Skip the line. Invite friends, unlock [perk]." | Get my link → form+share |
| 7 | `pricing` | Pricing / Checkout | convert on plans | nav · headline · toggle monthly/yearly · 3 plans · comparison table · guarantee · FAQ · footer | "Simple pricing that scales with you" | Choose [plan] |
| 8 | `demo` | Demo / Book a Call | booked meeting | nav · hero(calendar) · qualification(3 bullets) · agenda(3) · proof · FAQ · footer | "See [product] on your [data] in 20 minutes" | Book my demo → calendar |
| 9 | `webinar` | Event / Webinar | registration | nav · hero(date+countdown) · what you'll learn(3-4) · speaker · proof · register form · FAQ | "Free live training: [result] on [date]" | Save my seat → form |
| 10 | `app` | App Download | install | nav · hero(QR+badges) · screenshots(3) · features(3) · ratings · badges · footer | "[Product]: [result] in your pocket" | Download (App Store / Play) |
| 11 | `upsell` | Thank You / Upsell | second conversion | confirmation · next step(1) · upsell offer · proof · footer | "You're in! One more thing…" | Claim [upsell] / Go to dashboard |

Rules:
- Squeeze (4) and upsell (11): no nav links, no footer links beyond privacy.
- Sales (3): testimonial within one viewport of every price mention.
- Demo (8): calendar embed or 3-field form above the fold — never below.
- Waitlist (5) / viral (6): show live-feeling position ("#2,431 in line") even if static.
